How to Track Your Progress

Tracking progress helps you stay motivated by making long-term improvements easier to see.

  • Record how many minutes you walk or do cardio each week.
  • Write down sets, reps, and weights for strength exercises.
  • Note changes in energy levels, sleep, mood, and motivation.
  • Take progress photos every few weeks (optional).

You can track in a notebook, spreadsheet, or fitness app—whichever you’ll stick to consistently.
